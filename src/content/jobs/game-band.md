I was initially brought on to The Game Band to help with an unannounced Unity title that was in development for about a year before being shelved. I helped mainly with scaling the back-end systems that would ultimately support the multiplayer aspect of that game, but also got my first taste of building in Unity in a professional team setting. After the first project was shelved, I moved on to being the first developer moved fully onto the project that became *Dead Man's Party: A Knives Out Game*. My initial task for this game was adopting the Netflix SDK to support bi-directional communication between mobile devices and large screen devices. After that, I moved onto assisting with gameplay features that touched Unity systems like Timelines, FMOD for audio, and the Animate UI Materials package. I was also part of the team that helped localize the game to 12 different languages, working with audio engineers and QA to translate both audio and text for the game.
