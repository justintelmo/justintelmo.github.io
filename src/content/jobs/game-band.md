I joined The Game Band as the senior back-end engineer on an unannounced Unity title. I helped design and build its back end, worked on client-side gameplay, and built its CI/CD pipeline on Jenkins and AWS. It was also my first taste of building in Unity in a professional team setting. Then I became the first developer moved fully onto the project that became *Dead Man's Party: A Knives Out Game*. My initial task for this game was adopting the Netflix SDK to support bi-directional communication between mobile devices and large screen devices. After that, I moved onto assisting with gameplay features that touched Unity systems like Timelines, FMOD for audio, and the Animate UI Materials package. I was also part of the team that helped localize the game to 12 different languages, working with audio engineers and QA to translate both audio and text for the game.
