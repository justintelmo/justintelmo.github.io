At Glu Mobile, I worked on the server team for Covet Fashion, which at the time was a legacy PHP codebase that was a revenue driver for the company. Most of the work was balancing the modernization of the codebase with creating new features and establishing better team practices that would create a stronger foundation for the future of the game. It was here I got my first taste of building microservice architecture, which for me was helping pull a generic metrics system out of PHP into its own Kotlin codebase that other teams could use. This plus my experience integrating our internal subscription service into Covet Fashion were eye-opening and paradigm-shifting with regards to how to work with engineers across teams. I was here until shortly after the EA acquisition merged our team into EA Mobile in 2022.
